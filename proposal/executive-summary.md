Meridian Components has a working system that your operations team can no longer trust to change safely — and a previous engagement that stopped short of the finish line. Our goal is straightforward: leave you with a dashboard your ops team relies on, your IT team can confidently maintain, and your Tokyo warehouse can actually use.

## What we heard

The real constraint is not a list of features — it's a testing gap that has made your IT team a gatekeeper instead of an enabler. Until there is automated coverage on critical flows, every change carries risk that reasonable people are right to block. R3 is not one item among four; it is the unlock that makes everything else safe to deliver and safe to hand off.

Beyond that, your VP of Operations has waited long enough for restocking recommendations. R2 is not a "nice to have" — it is a concrete decision-support tool that reduces the manual work your team does every week to figure out what to order, for which warehouse, and within what budget.

## Our approach

We will work in three phases, each ending in a working, shippable increment — no cliff-hanger handoffs.

**Phase 1 — Stable foundation (weeks 1–3):** Architecture review (R4), full audit and remediation of the Reports module (R1), and initial browser test coverage (R3) for the core inventory and orders flows. At the end of this phase, your IT team has a test suite they can run, and the Reports page works.

**Phase 2 — New capability (weeks 4–7):** Restocking recommendations (R2), built on the existing data layer. Stock levels, demand forecast, and an operator-supplied budget ceiling feed a ranking model that surfaces purchase order recommendations by stockout risk — the metric your ops team cares about most. Test coverage extended to cover the new view.

**Phase 3 — Polish and handoff (weeks 8–9):** UI refresh proposal for your review (D1), i18n extension to remaining modules including Tokyo (D2), final documentation, and production-readiness review. Dark mode (D3) is scoped and available as an add-on if time allows.
